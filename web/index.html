Artist Collector

Every song by one artist, in one playlist.

To start, paste the Client ID of your Spotify app. You find it in the Developer Dashboard under Settings. It is saved only in this browser.

The Dashboard must list this Redirect URI:

Every song by one artist, in one playlist.

On Spotify an artist’s songs are spread across albums, singles and deluxe editions, and there is no button to play them all. Artist Collector reads the whole discography and saves every song in one playlist in your account. A song that appears on both a single and an album, or again as a remaster, is added only once.

Access is by invitation: the app is in Spotify’s development mode, so the email of your Spotify account must be on the owner’s list.

How it works

  1. A1
    Log in with Spotify

    The site asks to read your playlists and to create new ones. No password passes through it.

  2. A2
    Pick the artist

    Search by name and choose from the results.

  3. A3
    The discography is read

    Every album and single, one release at a time. A long career can take a few minutes.

  4. A4
    The playlist is saved

    It gets the artist’s name and appears in your library, ready to play or shuffle.
